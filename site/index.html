Look up from the screen.

Recess dims your Mac when it's time to rest your eyes, stretch or get up, and gets out of the way when you're back. If you've already walked off for a coffee, it noticed.

Download for Mac

Free and open source. For macOS 14 Sonoma and later, on Apple Silicon and Intel.

An hour with Recess

Out of the box you get two kinds of break: a few seconds every quarter hour to unclench and look across the room, and ten minutes each hour to properly get up. Change either, or add your own.

  1. A heads-up

    A minute before a longer break, a notification gives you time to finish your sentence.

  2. The screen dims

    A countdown, a calm theme and a suggestion. Postpone or skip if you really have to.

  3. Back to work

    The screen comes back by itself. Stepped away or closed the lid already? That counted as a break.

Small details that make it stick

Fits around your day

  • Start on time, wait until you stop typing, or ask first.
  • Only during work hours, only on weekdays, or timed from your calendar.
  • Holds off during video calls, presentations and fullscreen apps.
  • Time away from the keyboard and sleep count as rest.

Keeps you honest

  • Cap how many times a day you're allowed to postpone or skip.
  • Lock the buttons for the first few seconds of a break.
  • Optionally block Cmd-Tab and Force Quit while a break is on.
  • A chart of breaks taken, postponed and skipped.

Yours to shape

  • Break screens with a breathing guide, clocks, eye and desk stretches, or your own text, photos or video.
  • Sounds, spoken reminders and your own AppleScript or shell scripts.
  • A countdown in the menu bar and global keyboard shortcuts.
  • recess:// links for Shortcuts and automation.

Installing, and the one-time prompt

Recess isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS asks you to confirm. It takes ten seconds, and you only do it once.

  1. Open Recess.dmg and drag Recess into Applications.
  2. Open Recess. When macOS says it can't verify the app, click Done.
  3. In System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm with Touch ID or your password, then click Open.
Rather use Terminal? 
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Recess.app
open /Applications/Recess.app

Nothing leaves your Mac

No account, no analytics, and no network requests of its own. Your settings are plain files in your Library folder. Recess is MIT licensed, so you can read every line, or build it yourself: 

git clone https://github.com/NeelkanthTandel/recess.git
cd recess && make install

Recess is free software under the MIT License. Set in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a typeface designed by the Braille Institute for easier reading.
